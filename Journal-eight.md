## Design
low fidelity prototype

Design prototype#1
The first design is an improved iteration of our initial model. It features a more rectangular shape with heavily rounded edges, combining elements of our original design with Nadia’s vision of incorporating the appearance of wax cylinders. Since this version is already thicker, we decided to incorporate a handle into the design.

Design:
*edges on the design will be more rounded*
*velcro handle will be added*
Sensor opening on the bottom



Design prototype#2
The design intentionally imitates the appearance of the wax cylinder that inspired her project.
Since the design will have components inside, the box will have to be more elongated in order for the design to appear more visually nice rather than bulky.
Suggestion regarding the color of the box: Color can remain black or to make it seems as though it’s part of the art piece, we can print it in one of the colors of the tapestry.

Design: 
sensor will be on the bottom - flat surface
Potential addition: handle

 Design prototype#3
The design is also cylindrical in shape to replicate the appearance of traditional wax cylinders. The base is flatter, as Nadia mentioned that she would prefer to hold the object by its sides rather than like a flashlight. To maintain her original vision while accommodating this preference, we decided to incorporate a handle instead of adding grips to the sides.
Design:
No flat surface
 Opening that keeps sensor in place; walls of opening recessed inwards, so it still has the cylinder shape while keeping the sensor stable in place.
