## Task Description - 3D Model
Me and Sophie were tasked with working on the enclosure for the sensor. Essentially we had to 3D design a box to put the Arduino, that would then read the colors and output sound. 

## 3D Model finalized design
Update from last week: 
- Finalized the design: We were between a rectangle shaped box or a semi cylinder shaped. After careful considering, since the box containing the sensors will be held, it would be highly uncomfortable to hold as the edges would be too sharp if the design was a rectangle, therefore we went with the rounded cylinder top as it is easier to hold, while maintaining the surface flat.

Final draft design to show at consultation:

## Notes from 3D Consultation
We went with the rounded cylinder like design and the guy from the 3D printing advised that having that specific design would be it difficult to pick up, as the bottom surface is flat. He suggested the addition of some sort of grip to the sides of the cylinder, so we went with that option.

## Modifications
Once the design was printed and we tested it with the sensors, a few changes we saw was first the hole for the sensor was a little too small. However it still worked, just for future reference for the final project we would make it a little bigger. The rounded design did help with holding it but the look of it felt and looked very awkward and one teammate commented it looked like a "coffin" so we would be changing the design for the final. We quickly brainstormed some simple potential ideas of what it can look like. I added that perhaps if we made it a cylinder - (referencing one of Nadia's props she provided for inspo the wax cylinder) without the flat surface or the grip and we make it simply stand upright so it can be easily picked up.
